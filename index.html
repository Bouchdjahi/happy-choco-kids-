WHERE HAPPINESS MEETS CHOCOLATE

SINCE 2021 · PALMARY FOOD

Born in Algeria. Made for joy.

Happy Choco Kids was launched in 2021 as part of Palmary's HAPPY brand. Palmary Food, founded in 2001 in Boumerdès, Algeria, has grown to become one of the country's leading food companies, exporting to over 30 countries.

Happy Choco Kids quickly became a sensation. Algerians call it "the Algerian Kinder Bueno" — but at three times less the price, it's become a favorite for its own unique taste. In 2022, it was voted Best Product of the Year in Algeria.

Every bar contains four simple ingredients: creamy milk chocolate, crunchy wafer, flavorful hazelnuts — and a hidden ingredient that isn't listed but you'll feel instantly: happiness.
